About Minit Infotech

Connecting Businesses with Technology

Minit Infotech is an India-based B2B mobile phone and electronics supplier, established in 2025. Based in Delhi NCR, we serve retailers, wholesalers, resellers and corporate buyers across India.

Who We Are

Minit Infotech focuses on helping businesses source mobile phones, accessories and smart devices across multiple brands.

We understand that product availability, competitive commercial terms and dependable communication are important when sourcing technology products for a business.

Our approach is centred on understanding your requirements, discussing suitable sourcing options and coordinating the order process with clarity.

Whether you are a mobile retailer looking for smartphones, a wholesaler exploring bulk requirements or a business sourcing devices for institutional use, Minit Infotech provides a single point of contact for your product enquiries.

Our Mission

Making Technology Sourcing Simpler for Businesses

Our aim is to make the product sourcing process straightforward for business buyers through clear communication, requirement-based enquiries and transparent commercial discussions.

What We Do

  • Mobile phone sourcing and supply enquiries.
  • iPhone and smartphone enquiries.
  • Mobile accessories.
  • Smart devices and wearables.
  • Bulk and corporate product requirements.

Our Approach

  1. Understand the Requirement

    We begin by understanding the products, brands, models and quantities required.

  2. Discuss Available Options

    We review the requirement and communicate relevant sourcing options, availability and pricing.

  3. Keep the Process Clear

    We believe in clear quotations, agreed commercial terms and proper order documentation.
