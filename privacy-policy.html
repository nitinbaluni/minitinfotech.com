Minit Infotech respects the privacy of individuals and businesses visiting our website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and handle information shared with us through our website and business communication channels.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry through our website, we may collect information such as:

  • Name.
  • Company or business name.
  • Business email address.
  • Phone number.
  • Business type.
  • Product category and brand preferences.
  • Product specifications and quantity requirements.
  • Delivery location.
  • Other information shared in your message.

We may also collect certain technical information through website cookies and analytics tools, depending on the tools implemented on our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information provided to:

  • Respond to business and product enquiries.
  • Share product availability and commercial information.
  • Communicate regarding quotations and orders.
  • Coordinate order-related activities.
  • Improve our website and customer communication.
  • Maintain necessary business records.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information submitted through our website.

Where necessary for responding to an enquiry or processing an order, information may be shared with relevant service providers, business partners or logistics providers, subject to applicable requirements.

5. Data Retention

We retain enquiry and business communication records for as long as necessary for the purposes for which they were collected, including business operations, legal compliance and record-keeping.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable laws, you may contact us to request information about personal data held by us, request correction of inaccurate information or raise concerns about its use.
